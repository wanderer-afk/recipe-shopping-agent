# Creamy Chicken Onion Pasta

Chicken and onions in a creamy Parmesan sauce over penne. Makes 6 plates.

## Method
1. Cook the chicken fully in oil, cut it into pieces, and set aside (165 F / 74 C).
2. Slowly cook the sliced onions in butter until soft and golden. Add garlic, flour, wine, stock and lemon juice; simmer.
3. Add cream, Parmesan and spices. Return the chicken, toss with cooked penne, and serve.
