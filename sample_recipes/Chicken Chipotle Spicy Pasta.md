# Chicken Chipotle Spicy Pasta

Penne in a smoky chipotle cream sauce with chicken, peppers and peas. Makes 6 plates.

## Method
1. Season the chicken with the spices and cook in oil until cooked through (165 F / 74 C). Cut into bites and set aside.
2. Cook the onion and pepper, then add garlic, chipotles with a little sauce, broth and cream. Simmer until thick.
3. Add Parmesan and peas, toss with cooked penne and chicken, and serve.
