# Creamy Lemon Garlic Chicken with Rice

Chicken in a lemon garlic cream sauce, served with rice. Makes 6 plates.

## Method
1. Cook the rice. Season and sear the chicken in oil until cooked through (165 F / 74 C). Set aside.
2. In the same pan, soften the onion and garlic in butter, add wine and stock, and simmer until reduced.
3. Stir in cream and lemon juice. Thicken with the cornstarch mixed in cold water. Return the chicken, warm through, and serve over the rice.
