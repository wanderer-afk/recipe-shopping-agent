# Baked Ziti with Meat Sauce

Ziti baked with a meat sauce, ricotta and two cheeses. Makes 6 plates.

## Method
1. Brown the beef and sausage with the onion and garlic until no pink remains (160 F / 71 C). Stir in the tomato paste, tomatoes, herbs, sugar and salt. Simmer 30 minutes.
2. Cook the ziti until just tender, drain, and mix with the sauce.
3. Mix the ricotta with the egg and some Parmesan. Layer ziti, ricotta and mozzarella in a dish, top with cheese, cover and bake at 375 F (190 C) for 25 minutes, then uncover until bubbly and the center reaches 165 F / 74 C.
