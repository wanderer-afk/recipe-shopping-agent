# Marry Me Chicken Pasta

Chicken and penne in a creamy sun-dried tomato and Parmesan sauce. Makes 6 plates.

## Method
1. Season the chicken and cook in oil until cooked through (165 F / 74 C). Slice and set aside.
2. Cook garlic and sun-dried tomatoes, add broth and simmer, then add cream and Parmesan until smooth.
3. Add herbs and seasoning, toss with cooked penne and chicken, and serve.
