# California Breakfast Burritos

Breakfast burritos with eggs, hash browns, bacon and cheese, made for the freezer. Makes 6 burritos.

## Method
1. Cook the bacon until crisp, drain, and chop. Fry the hash browns in oil until golden and set aside.
2. Whisk the eggs with milk, salt and spices, then scramble them gently in butter until just set (160 F / 71 C).
3. Fill each tortilla with eggs, potatoes, bacon and cheese. Roll tightly in foil, cool, and freeze. Reheat wrapped until hot all the way through (165 F / 74 C).
