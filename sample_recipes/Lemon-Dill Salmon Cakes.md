# Lemon-Dill Salmon Cakes

Canned salmon patties with lemon, dill and panko, baked until golden. Makes 6 plates.

## Method
1. Drain the salmon and flake it. Mix with the eggs, mayonnaise, mustard, minced onion and garlic, dill, lemon zest, and half the panko.
2. Shape into patties, coat in the rest of the panko, and chill 30 minutes.
3. Bake at 425 F (220 C) until golden and hot inside (165 F / 74 C). Serve with lemon wedges.
