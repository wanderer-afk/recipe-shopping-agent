# Garlic Teriyaki Chicken with Green Beans and Rice

Chicken thighs in a sweet-savory garlic and ginger glaze, served with rice and green beans. Makes 6 plates.

## Method
1. Simmer the soy sauce, mirin, sugar, garlic and ginger for about 5 minutes until slightly thick.
2. Brown the chicken in oil in batches, then add the sauce and simmer until the chicken is cooked through (165 F / 74 C).
3. Cook the rice and the green beans separately. Serve together.
