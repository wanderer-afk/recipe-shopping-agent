# Chicken Fajita Empanadas

Baked empanadas filled with chicken and peppers in fajita spices. Makes about 24 (6 plates).

## Method
1. Make the dough: rub cold butter into the flour and salt, add egg and vinegar, bring it together, and chill 1 hour.
2. Cook the chicken, peppers and onion with the spices until the chicken reaches 165 F / 74 C and the pan is dry. Stir in lime and cheese, then cool.
3. Roll the dough thin, cut circles, fill, fold and crimp. Brush with beaten egg and bake at 400 F (200 C) for about 20 to 25 minutes, until golden. Cool before freezing.
