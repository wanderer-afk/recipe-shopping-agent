# Garlic Butter Chicken and Creamy Potatoes

Chicken thighs in garlic butter, served on creamy mashed potatoes. Makes 6 plates.

## Method
1. Season the chicken and sear it in oil until golden. Lower the heat, add butter and garlic, and baste until cooked through (165 F / 74 C).
2. Deglaze with the wine, add the stock and simmer briefly.
3. Boil the potatoes until soft, mash with cream cheese, cream, butter and salt. Serve the chicken over the potatoes with the pan sauce and a squeeze of lemon.
