Our Kitchen Grocery Book

Give it a recipe. Say what is at home. Get the list to buy.

1. Add a recipe

Type or paste the ingredients, one per line, amount first. You can also paste text copied from a PDF or a list. For example: 1 1/2 cups milk, 2 eggs, 3 tbsp butter. If a spoon or cup amount is sold by weight, add the grams in brackets: 3/4 cup rice (150 g).

Or add a recipe as a photo or a PDF. Choose a file, or take a photo with your phone camera. The text is read on your own device. The photo is not uploaded anywhere. Then check the words it found and fix any mistakes before you press Check.

Add something to the list

Type one item, like milk, and press Add to list. No recipe needed.
